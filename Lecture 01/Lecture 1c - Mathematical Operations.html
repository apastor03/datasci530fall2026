Create an array from a list

vec_b  = np.array([0,1,0])
vec_c  = np.array([10,100,1000,2000,5000])

IV. Random Numbers ¶

Why randomness?

  • Simulate different scenarios: high risk or low risk
  • Study properties of a complex system and/or estimator
  • In medicine, randomly assign subjects to treatment or control

Create a vector of random variables

In [17]:
# This code creates a vector of random variables 
# generated from a normal distribution with
# mean "loc" (location) and standard deviation "scale"
# the number of distinct variabels is "size"

randomvar_a = np.random.normal(loc=0, scale=1, size=10)
print(randomvar_a)

[-0.47438742  0.74051728 -0.32615516 -1.4361001   0.02754296 -0.68772772
 -0.15578776  0.49562504  0.62826679  0.33773758]

Random numbers differ every time!

  • Avoid this problem by drawing from a "pregenerated" set.
  • This is called a seed

Matrix multiplication

$ XY = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \\ 0 & 0 & 6 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 4 \\ 5 & 4 \\ 6 & 0 \end{pmatrix} $
